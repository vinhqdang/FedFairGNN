\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Fairness in graph neural networks.}
GNNs inherit and amplify bias through homophilous message passing. Remedies
include adversarial debiasing (FairGNN \citep{dai2021say}), counterfactual and
stability regularisation (NIFTY \citep{agarwal2021nifty}), pre-processing that
minimises inter-group distributional distance (EDITS \citep{dong2022edits}),
sensitive-information neutralisation via heterogeneous neighbours (FairSIN
\citep{yang2024fairsin}), re-balancing through counterfactual mixup (FairGB
\citep{li2024fairgb}), invariant learning across inferred sensitive partitions
(FairINV \citep{zhu2024fairinv}), and disentangled debiasing (DAB-GNN
\citep{lee2025dabgnn}). All assume \emph{centralised} data. Our FSER is closest
in spirit to the edge-reweighting variants of FairSIN and to distribution-aware
reweighting \citep{fnrgnn2025}, but operates inside a federated, private, and
robust pipeline.

\paragraph{Fairness in federated learning.}
Two notions coexist. \emph{Client (performance) fairness} equalises accuracy
across clients: q-FedAvg \citep{li2020fair} up-weights high-loss clients.
\emph{Group fairness} equalises outcomes across demographic groups: FairFed
\citep{ezzeldin2023fairfed} adjusts aggregation weights by each client's local
fairness gap; FedFB ports FairBatch to FL; PUFFLE \citep{corbucci2024puffle}
blends a demographic-parity loss with DP-SGD under a target-disparity
controller; FedFACT \citep{fedfact2025} gives provable global-and-local group
fairness. These target tabular or image data. For \emph{graphs}, F$^2$GNN
\citep{f2gnn2023} combines a fairness-weighted aggregation with structure-aware
group-balance weights, and very recent work (FairGFL, 2025) adds DP to federated
fair GNNs. We compare against FairFed, q-FedAvg, FedFB, and F$^2$GNN, and against
FairSIN run federated.

\paragraph{Differential privacy in FL.}
DP-FedAvg \citep{mcmahan2018learning} clips per-client updates and adds Gaussian
noise; privacy is tracked with the moments accountant / R\'enyi DP
\citep{mironov2017renyi,abadi2016deep}. On the compact GNNs used for tabular
graphs, isotropic gradient noise at meaningful $\epsilon$ overwhelms the signal
(Section~\ref{sec:exp-privacy}). FedFDP \citep{ling2024feddp} adapts clipping to
fairness but still privatises the whole gradient. FTGD instead privatises only
the \emph{sufficient statistics} of the fairness term, exploiting the fact that
the sensitive attribute never enters the task loss---a targeted mechanism whose
$(\epsilon,\delta)$ guarantee follows from the Gaussian mechanism and
post-processing immunity.

\paragraph{Byzantine-robust aggregation.}
Krum and Multi-Krum \citep{blanchard2017krum}, coordinate-wise median and
trimmed mean \citep{yin2018byzantine}, Bulyan \citep{mhamdi2018hidden}, and
trust-bootstrapping schemes such as FLTrust \citep{cao2021fltrust} defend
against malicious updates under attacks including sign-flipping,
model-replacement \citep{bagdasaryan2020backdoor}, ALIE \citep{baruch2019little},
and IPM \citep{xie2019fall}. None targets fairness. Recently, fairness-poisoning
attacks emerged---EAB-FL \citep{eabfl2024} and fairness-constrained
manipulation---that \emph{increase} disparity while preserving accuracy, evading
both robust and fairness-aware defences. We reproduce a fairness-poisoning
threat and defend with a robust fairness-constrained aggregator.

\paragraph{Explainability, uncertainty, and trust metrics.}
GNN explainers attribute predictions to edges/features; we use the FSER
attention itself as a native, audit-ready explanation and add gradient-based
attribution of the \emph{disparity}. Predictive uncertainty via MC-dropout
\citep{gal2016dropout} and calibration (ECE, Brier) quantify confidence
reliability. Composite trust scoring for FL is nascent; we contribute a
transparent power-mean aggregation over utility, fairness, privacy, robustness,
and calibration. Finally, we align the system to the EU AI Act and NIST AI RMF,
operationalising accountability as an auditable artifact rather than prose.

\paragraph{Positioning.}
No prior method unifies fairness, differential privacy, and Byzantine robustness
for federated GNNs, nor evaluates on large-scale crypto graphs. FedFairGNN does
both, and reports the full trustworthiness picture---including uncertainty,
explainability, sustainability, and regulatory mapping---under one framework.
